# Onnex TMS - Transport Management System

The Onnex TMS is the foundational layer of our ecosystem, providing drivers with the tools they need to manage and grow their delivery businesses efficiently.&#x20;

Key features include:

* Job Matching: Seamlessly connect drivers with clients needing reliable delivery services. Allow drivers and clients to work independently of the CoOp but to have it as a back up for those busy days.
* Business Growth Tools: Offer resources for drivers to establish and scale their own delivery businesses, leveraging tools for scheduling, route optimization, customer engagement, billing & reporting.
* Driver Empowerment: Enable drivers to maintain autonomy while benefiting from a structured system that supports scalability and success.

By democratizing access to these tools, the Onnex TMS ensures that drivers remain at the forefront of the delivery economy, directly benefiting from the industry’s growth.

Another frame of reference that may be useful to some, is that we are basically taking the Over-the-Road (OTR) trucking business model and applying that to the last-mile business so that issues of ‘independent contractors’ in states like California with AB5 are like dust in the rearview mirror. Our drivers are truly self-employed and simply using our platform like any other software tool they need to run their business.
