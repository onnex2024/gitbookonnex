# Onnex Payment Guarantee

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Onnex Payment Guarantee.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Financial stability is critical for drivers and businesses alike. The Onnex Payment Guarantee ensures that every delivery transaction is secure, reliable, and efficient. This component includes:

* Payment Assurance: Drivers are guaranteed prompt and secure payment upon successful delivery, eliminating financial uncertainties.
* Comprehensive Payment Acceptance: Support for all payment types, including checks and cryptocurrencies, via a secure system.
* Revenue Opportunities: Drivers can act as payment aggregators, creating additional revenue streams.
* Efficiency and Trust: Streamlined electronic transactions reduce costs and build confidence among all stakeholders.

The Payment Guarantee is a cornerstone of Onnex’s mission to provide financial security and competitive pay to drivers while enhancing reliability for businesses.
