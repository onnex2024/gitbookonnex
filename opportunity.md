# Opportunity

Gig-work is broken, all the power and relationships are in the hands of large tech companies, so we simply wanted to change that. &#x20;

Our vision is to create a robust, decentralized ecosystem that empowers drivers, delivery professionals, and businesses to succeed in an environment characterized by transparency, innovation, and fairness. Rather than these being hollow words, here is how we are going about making this a reality.

1. All our clients / shippers can use our platform for free to manage any drivers they currently employ, and simply use our service for extra deliveries they don’t have the manpower for.
2. Our delivery platform is totally open source, see for yourself: link to github. In this way we are encouraging delivery companies to use our foundational software that they can develop their own workflow and methodologies on top of.
3. The Onnex platform will supply stops and deliveries to qualified drivers, but it will also allow drivers to have their own clients on the platform and to use the Onnex CoOp as a backup when needed.
4. Drivers will be paid a 1% override on revenue that they bring in from new clients that is transported on the CoOp platform.&#x20;
5. Clients will be paid a 1% discount (or fee) for any revenue they refer on to the program.
