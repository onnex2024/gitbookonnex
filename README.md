# Overview

<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Overview.png" alt="" width="145"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

This Onnex overview offers an in-depth explanation of the vision, technology and business models that powers both the real world asset, Onnex Inc, a last-mile B2B delivery company, and its virtual counterpart, OnnexDAO.

Onnex Inc, is a slightly unusual same day delivery company as it does not have a centralized warehouse or offices overseeing it operations, instead it has been built as a low overhead decentralized business that is uniquely 'driver centric' in its approach. The name Onnex reflects the businesses delivery roots as it is a simple shortening of ‘onto the next’ delivery.

Therefore it was only natural that this business opportunity would flourish when combined with the natural attributes of a DAO: increased communication, transparency and web3 blockchain technology.
