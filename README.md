# Welcome

This Onnex White Paper offers an in-depth explanation of the vision, technology and business models that powers both the real world asset, Onnex Inc, a last-mile B2B delivery company, and it's virtual counterpart, OnnexDAO.

Onnex Inc, is a slightly unusual same day delivery company as it does not have a centralized warehouse or offices overseeing it operations, instead it has been build as a low overhead decentralized business that is uniquely 'driver centric' in its approach.

Therefore it was only natural that this business opportunity when combined with the increased communication, transparency and web3 blockchain technology provided by a decentralized autonomous organization, or DAO, would flourish.
