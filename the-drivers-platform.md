# 'The Driver's Platform'



<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/The Drivers Platform.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Our main motivations for starting Onnex were:

1. We knew we could do same-day delivery better than others.&#x20;
2. We knew that there was real demand for this service.
3. We believe drivers deserved better treatment and should share in the success of the business.
4. We had a vision to turn this into a national and perhaps an international company.

We take pride in being unwaveringly transparent in every aspect of our business, with our customers, contractors (drivers) and employees. We hope that it is our great service added to our grounded sense of fairness in everything we do that keeps our clients and you happy.
