# USPs for Drivers

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/USPs for Drivers.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

1. A full day of work - we prioritize our drivers on seniority (when you came on to the platform) and on performance metrics. For our oldest and best drivers we aim to always give them the first choice of accepting as much work as they want. This is the system working for you, not you for the system.
2. Your own business - as with any business starting it can be hard, so we’ll always be an option for you to get started with delivering off our platform, but you can also bring your own customers and combine working with them along with picking up stops out of our system.
3. Set your own pricing - the only way to truly run your own business is to be able to choose the hours you want to work and the price you are willing to work for. The Onnex platform gives you that option.
4. We always have work, but we generally organize our system into three runs a day - 8.30am to 12.30pm - 1pm to 5pm - 5.30pm to 9.30pm. There is effectively a 30 mins break between ‘shifts’ should you need to take a break.
5. Being paid by the hour or by the stop - we know that sometimes traffic, weather, or something unforeseen can seriously affect anyone’s ability to deliver a package, therefore what was once a profitable stop now costs you money. So we have a payment floor of an hourly wage that we will pay you the higher of your stop rate or the hourly rate so you always have a living wage.
6. Advanced software integrates with all 3rd party applications
