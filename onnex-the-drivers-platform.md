# Onnex: 'the driver's platform'

Our main motivations for starting Onnex was threefold:

1. We knew we could do same-day delivery better than others&#x20;
2. We knew that there was real demand for this service.
3. We believed drivers could be treated better and could participate in the success of the business.
4. We had a vision to turn this into a national and perhaps international company.

We take pride in being unwaveringly transparent in every aspect of our business, with our customers, vendors (drivers) and with our employees. We hope that it is our great service added to our grounded sense of fairness in everything we do that keeps our clients and you happy.
